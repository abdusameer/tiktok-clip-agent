# /make-content (unattended)

Arguments: `$ARGUMENTS`

Policy: `CONTENT_EDITING_RULES.md` is canonical and overrides anything in this file (runtime is never a rule; ranking count is honest, min 3, never padded; ranking AND standalone passes are both mandatory; build the candidate ledger). Forms: `--drop media in sourcevideo/ then content-intake` · `--video <path>` · `--video <path> --audio <path>` · `--source-id <id>`. Once invoked this runs start to finish: NO questions about clips, categories, titles, boundaries, colors or formats; NO intermediate plans or approvals. When uncertain apply the deterministic rules, skip the candidate, send an uncertain render to `needs-review/` and keep going. `/make-rankings` stays available for ranking-only production; do not create other commands.

Setup for every shell call: `export PATH=/opt/homebrew/bin:$PATH`; CLI: `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m clipagent.cli <cmd>`.
Never: upload, publish, transfer to TikTok, spend credits, install software, permanently delete, purge a source, or touch anything outside the project / this run. Moving allowlisted temporary files to the macOS Trash is allowed.

## Layout (one clean structure)
`source_library/<id>/{master.mp4, source.json, transcript/, analysis/{timeline.json, segments.csv, candidates.json, rejected.json, contact-sheets/, thumbnails/}}` · `runs/<run-id>/{STATUS.md, status.json, ingest/, segments/shortlisted-previews/, plan/, working/{rankings,funny-moments,continuous-moments}/, qa/{reports,frames}/, logs/, usage.json}` · `approved_clips/rankings/<run-id>/R01-<name>/` · `approved_clips/standalone/funny/<run-id>/F01-<name>/` · `approved_clips/standalone/continuous/<run-id>/C01-<name>/` (each: `<ID>-<name>.mp4, cover.jpg, caption.txt, manifest.json, qa.json`) · `approved_clips/_reports/<source-id>/<run-id>/{index.html, summary.json}` · `needs-review/<run-id>/<output>/` · `logs/<run-id>-report.txt` · inbox `sourcevideo/`. Filenames are descriptive (`R01-funniest-reactions.mp4`); hashes live in manifests. One canonical `master.mp4` per source, never copied.

## Workflow
1. `doctor` (report, never install). `content-intake` (scans `sourcevideo/`, pairs video+audio, ingests, starts a run) or `content-start --source-id <id>` or `--video <path> [--audio <path>]` -> prints the run id (new media is ingested into one master; identity matches stop with exit 2). STATUS.md/status.json now track the stage: INGESTING -> ANALYZING -> CLASSIFYING -> PLANNING -> RENDERING -> QA -> ORGANIZING -> CLEANING -> COMPLETE (or FAILED).
2. `content-segment <run-id>`: full analysis (reused if present), then segment detection. The source is usually an edited compilation: every hard cut is scored; a camera-angle change inside one continuing event (same palette, ambience/level, speech across) does NOT split a segment; a real clip boundary does. Every second of the timeline is a segment or a rejected section with a reason (`timeline.json`); `segments.csv` lists ID, times, description, transcript, visible action, boundary confidence, humor score, context score, format, rejection reason, used-clip match; each segment has a 3-frame strip in `analysis/thumbnails/` and contact sheets in `analysis/contact-sheets/`. If very few segments come out of a long compilation a second coverage pass runs automatically.
3. **Edit as an adaptive editor (visual review).** Read `CONTENT_EDITING_RULES.md` (section 10: recovery pass) and `research.md` first. `content-segment` also writes `analysis/audio_events.json` and `analysis/review_evidence.json` (full transcript, audio-event summary and neighbours per segment): READ them with the contact sheets - a contact sheet can never prove an audio joke, so never reject on that basis; reconstruct each scene from its neighbours, merge across segment ids, and apply the safe transformations before rejecting anything. After planning, `contentflow.reconcile_ledger` must account for every segment exactly once. Read `analysis/contact-sheets/segments_*.jpg` (and strips/frames when needed), then write `reviews.json` and run `apply-reviews <source-id> reviews.json`.
   **Freedom rule:** you may choose format, pacing, framing, captions, effects and structure (cold opens, teasers, replays, freeze frames, punch-ins, speed changes, SFX, highlighted words), but every choice must serve comprehension, anticipation, humor, reaction or retention, stay truthful, and every trim needs a recorded reason. Length is never a goal: a moment lasts exactly as long as its complete setup, escalation, payoff, reaction and necessary aftermath need, so no shortening or padding to hit a target. `duration_reason` records why the chosen span is COMPLETE, never why it is short or long enough.
   **Events, not shots.** A hard cut or camera change is not an event boundary; a change of action, conversation, goal or people is. Merge adjacent shots into one event while the same unresolved setup continues. Map each event as HOOK, SETUP, ESCALATION, TURN, PAYOFF, REACTION, AFTERSHOCK, then choose start/end (`use_start/use_end`) only after seeing the whole sequence. Never start mid-word; keep the reaction; stop after the strongest beat.
   **Treatments considered per event** (record the one chosen and why, plus why the runner-up lost): minimal continuous; tightened continuous; standalone punchline; reaction-led; context headline; cold open then context; full developing story; ranking/themed montage; rejection. If two versions are genuinely strong you may emit a tight cut and a fuller-context cut only through the recorded `standalone_reuse` (ranking + standalone), never as two same-kind videos; they must differ meaningfully.
   **Treatment by moment type:** instant visual gag - enter right before the trigger, end after the reaction. Fast banter - drop repetition, keep both sides. Awkward/cringe - keep the silence and the face. Chaotic action - keep the action understandable. Story/escalation - preserve chronology and reversals. Gameplay reaction - show trigger and face. Performance - fewer cuts. Ranking - only independent moments sharing one strong, specific, truthful theme; a ranking wrapper alone creates no attention, so never put a standout standalone moment into a weaker ranking. Themed compilation - group repeated behavior without pretending it is a ranking.
   **Captions/effects:** one or two readable lines by visual density, phrase-timed (not word-flash), accurate wording, one accent color per video; keep captions off faces and action; no automatic zoom schedule, no random SFX, no fake laughs; wider framing when body language is the joke, close-up when the face is the payoff.
   **Originality gate:** reject or enrich any output that is only crop + caption + zoom of someone else's footage (YouTube down-ranks this). Each kept output needs real added value: a sharp headline/context framing, a deliberate structure, selection of the event, or substantive editing. Record it.
   **Format mapping for the engine.** The pipeline stores exactly one of `ranking`, `funny`, `continuous`, `rejected` per segment: use `funny` for standalone/short treatments, `continuous` for tightened, reaction-led, cold-open or full-story treatments, `ranking` for ranking/themed montages. The review itself carries the editorial record, and `content-plan` writes it to `plan/edit-log.json`. For `funny`/`continuous` reviews `apply-reviews` requires `treatment` (one of minimal_continuous, tightened_continuous, standalone_punchline, reaction_led, context_headline, cold_open_context, full_story, ranking_montage, themed_montage), `duration_reason`, `added_value`, plus `trim_reason` whenever `use_start/use_end` is set; `runner_up` is optional. Per review also provide: `visible_action, hook, setup, payoff, works_without_context, audio_confidence, visual_confidence, viewed_frames (>=3 own frames), title` (ranking: unique 2-5 words; funny/continuous: truthful 2-6 word headline), `category` (rankings; specific, not generic). Scores prioritize; they never auto-delete an understandable funny moment. Skip unverifiable candidates (`rejected` + reason); never ask.
4. `content-plan <run-id>`: RANKING PASS builds one ranking per narrow theme with every honest entry (3+, countdown to #1, unique titles, never padded; a theme above the header's technical limit is split into balanced parts), then the STANDALONE PASS makes a funny/continuous video from every remaining strong moment. Writes `plan/{rankings,funny-moments,continuous-moments,rejected}.json`, `projects/{rankings,standalone}/`, and `candidates/ledger.json` (every candidate, where it went or why not, both passes, intentional reuse). The permanent used-clips registry is checked. A moment may appear in both a ranking and a standalone only through a recorded `standalone_reuse` (new headline, materially different span, reason); near-identical timelines are refused. No duration rule exists; videos may run any length; no filler.
5. `content-render <run-id>`: per moment, two independent decodes (captions contain ONLY words both decodes confirm) and a whole-frame chat scan (severe slurs/hateful language block the moment; ordinary profanity stays uncensored; unreadable chat is recorded as unverified), then renders into `working/<format>/`. Rankings use the approved template; funny/continuous use the same crop, blur, font, label background, safe margins, audio and caption handling with only the headline pill (no badge). ONE seeded accent color per finished video (approved palette, contrast-checked, white fallback).
6. `content-finalize <run-id>`: technical, cut-boundary (no cut inside a spoken word), audio-sync, caption, label/headline, crop/framing, duplicate, text-safety and template QA; edit points are checked. Passed videos go to `approved_clips/rankings/<run>/`, `approved_clips/standalone/funny/<run>/` or `approved_clips/standalone/continuous/<run>/` with `status: ready_for_draft_transfer` (never "uploaded"/"published") and a full manifest (source timecodes, hook, payoff, editing decisions, runtime, candidate ids, reuse, QA) and their moments are registered as used; failed/uncertain ones (and byte-identical duplicate exports) go to `needs-review/<run>/<ID>/` with `FAILURE.txt`. A fixable failure (frozen/black frames or a cut inside a word at an edge) -> adjust `use_start/use_end` in the review, re-plan only that video and re-render under a fresh name (max 2 retries); then continue. Sloppy cuts must fail QA.
7. `content-clean <run-id>`: moves ONLY allowlisted temporary files (working renders and caches, shortlisted previews, QA frames, sampled evidence frames) to the Trash; preserves the master, transcript, timeline/segment catalog, classifications, contact sheets and thumbnails, registry, finals, manifests, QA reports, dashboard, source metadata, code/tests/skills/config. Rebuilds `index.html` (self-contained dashboard: source info, status, counts by format, a card per video with thumbnail, title, format, duration, source timestamps, rank count, QA, caption, cover title, accent color and a link to the MP4).
8. `content-report <run-id> --command "<exact command>"` is the ONE final report. Then STOP and wait.

## Completion boundary
Authorized without asking: local processing, QA, organizing, registry updates, recoverable Trash cleanup of allowlisted files. NOT authorized: credits, uploads, TikTok transfer, public publishing, permanent deletion, source purging.
